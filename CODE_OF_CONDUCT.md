# Code of Conduct

0docs follows a simple standard: be constructive, respectful, and focused on building useful software together.

## Expected behavior

- Be welcoming to contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Explain tradeoffs clearly and assume good intent.
- Keep discussions relevant to 0docs and documentation tooling.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, hate speech, or personal attacks.
- Doxxing or sharing private information.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions.
- Publishing secrets or exploiting security issues publicly before maintainers can respond.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
